MINUTES CHECK · WORD ADD-IN

Set up once. Review in Word.

Download the installation file, then upload it inside Word. You do not need to start a server.

  1. Open a document in Word in Chrome.
  2. Choose Home → Add-ins → More Settings → Upload My Add-in.
  3. Select the downloaded minutes-check-word.xml.
  4. Open Minutes Check, choose the meeting type, and start live Word review.
  5. Import the current PPTX deck to compare slide reports.

If custom add-in uploads are missing or blocked, your institution may need to approve the add-in. The document permission enables reading the body and applying only corrections you approve.

Test in a disposable document first. Confirm the beginning and end of the body appear and the preview updates after an edit. Live operation in your institution's Word account has not yet been verified.

Privacy and limitations

The application code is public. Document text and imported slides are processed in your browser by this application; they are not uploaded to an AI service. Microsoft Word retains its own normal cloud behavior. Chapter settings are saved locally. Review exports contain excerpts. Slide imports are snapshots, so re-import after changes. Images, headers, footers, and comments are not reviewed.
